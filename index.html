Before you pay

  • Check "Sold by"Buy from the Yonex brand store or a seller with a long rating history. Most of these listings are third-party sellers (for example BTPLD and RetailNet on Flipkart). Sunlight Sports is Yonex's India distributor.
  • Check the hologramScratch the hologram and verify its code at check.sunrise.com.sg. A code that's already marked used is a red flag. The serial number should be laser-engraved, not printed.
  • Weigh itA fake often weighs 5–10 g more or less than its stated class (4U ≈ 80–84 g). Check that it's stiff, the paint is even and the grommets are smooth.
  • Check strung vs unstrungPro rackets usually ship unstrung, so a ₹12–20k "strung Pro" listing is worth a second look. Stringing costs extra if you buy unstrung.
  • Wait if you canPrices often drop again later in the sale. The cheapest rackets (GR 303i, ZR 100, Nanoflare 8000i) are the ones most often copied.
  • Keep the invoiceThe Yonex India warranty depends on the invoice from an authorised seller. A 7-day replacement window is common on Flipkart.
